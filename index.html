Our consultation begins with an in-depth review of your personal, academic, and professional background against current immigration requirements. We identify eligible pathways, outline the complete documentation checklist, and provide clear timeline estimates so you can make informed decisions.

Document requirements vary significantly based on destination country and visa stream. Typically, this includes a valid international passport, academic credentials, verified employment records, financial solvency statements, and medical/police certificates. We compile a customized checklist during your eligibility review.

Processing timelines are established solely by the relevant national immigration departments and embassies. Timelines range from several weeks for tourist visas to several months for permanent residency or skilled migration streams.
